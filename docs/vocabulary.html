Vocabulary

The closed word lists for every slot. A word that is not listed here does not belong in a name.

Categories and properties

The category is the first word after the owner; the property says what inside the category the token sets. shadow and opacity have no property; the role follows the category directly.

CategoryPropertiesComponents may also use

Component sizes use logical axes like CSS inline-size and block-size: in horizontal text inline is the width and block the height. --d-comp-DModal-size-max-inline is the modal's max width.

Roles

A role says what a value is for. In --d-sys-color-text-muted, muted is the role. If a property has roles, the role is always written, and the neutral one is default. Properties that are pure scales (font-size, space-*, border-radius) have no role and go straight to the variant.

If a property has roles, the role is always written, and the neutral one is default.

A system token always describes a purpose or level, never a UI component: shadow-overlay, not shadow-dropdown. The one exception is input, because form fields share one look across every component that has them.

Intent words today: accent danger success warning info highlight love. accent replaces tertiary. warning and info have no color of their own in core yet. Today warnings borrow the highlight color (--highlight-bg) and info borrows the accent (--tertiary-low). Their tokens can start with those values and get their own colors when the base colors are redesigned.

Roles by property

PropertyRoles

In components

Components put their kind in the role slot (DButton: default, primary, danger, success, flat, transparent), as in --d-comp-DButton-color-background-primary-hovered. Spacing tokens can put an axis there instead: inline, block, inline-start, inline-end, block-start or block-end, as in --d-comp-SidebarSectionLink-space-padding-inline.

Variants

A variant is a step on a scale. There are four scales, and each property uses at most one of them.

KindWordsUsed byRules
size3xs 2xs xs sm md lg xl 2xl 3xl 4xl 5xlfont-size, size-*, border-radius, border-width, motion-durationOne scale for everything except spacing. md is the middle step. Never large, small or default. border-radius also takes full.
stephalf 1 to 12space-*Counts 4px units: 2 is 8px, 5 is 20px. Spacing needs finer steps than size words give, like core's current --space-N and most larger systems. See Space scale.

Only accent has a near-complete ramp in core today. Danger and success have about two steps, love has one. warning and info have no ramp of their own; they borrow highlight and accent steps. The grid stays complete so names are predictable, but a token is only published when it has a value.

Color values

A color token always holds a full color. There are no -rgb twins (--secondary-rgb and similar, which exist today only to add transparency). Apply transparency with color-mix(): color-mix(in srgb, var(--d-sys-color-background-default) 80%, transparent).
